# Source Matrix

Different topic types should prefer different source mixes.

## Product Topic

- official release notes
- product docs
- real user discussion

## Company Topic

- official announcements
- interviews
- reporting with direct attribution

## Model Or Technical Topic

- paper or technical post
- official model note
- informed practitioner analysis

## Trend Or Phenomenon Topic

- multiple social signals
- original examples
- commentary that explains why the behavior matters

Do not rely on a single aggregator when a stronger original source exists.
