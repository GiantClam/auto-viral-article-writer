# Angle Selection

Research should not end with "here are some facts". It should produce a usable angle.

## Good Angle Qualities

- specific enough to support a real title
- narrow enough to write clearly
- grounded in the available sources
- meaningful to the target audience

## Weak Angle Signs

- too generic
- too dependent on hype language
- stronger than the source material can support
- indistinguishable from yesterday's AI article

When multiple angles exist, prefer the one that is both supported and differentiated.
