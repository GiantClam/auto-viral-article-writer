# Brief Strength Levels

Use these levels to decide whether a topic is ready to move into drafting.

## L0 - Signal Only

- only chatter or a thin headline
- not ready for an article brief

## L1 - Single Source

- one source exists
- enough to note the topic, not enough for a strong article

## L2 - Multi-Signal

- multiple signals or sources exist
- enough to form a working article brief with caution notes

## L3 - Article Ready

- source-backed angle is clear
- enough detail exists to support section planning

## L4 - Deep Analysis Ready

- enough breadth and depth exist for a much longer or more analytical piece

Prefer to move into `write-article` at L2 or above. Prefer L3 for confident drafting.
