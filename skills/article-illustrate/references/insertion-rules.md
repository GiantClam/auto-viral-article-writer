# Insertion Rules

Only insert illustrations in the article body after the `---` divider.

## Eligible Headings

- bold section headings like `**Section Name**`

## Exclusions

- title candidate lines beginning with `#`
- sections with no real body content
- numbered items like `**1. xxx**`

## Insertion Behavior

- one image per selected position
- exact heading match
- alt text derived from the section name
