# Status Levels

Use these levels when reporting article-family readiness.

## A0 - Topic Only

- no persisted brief
- no draft

## A1 - Brief Ready

- brief exists
- draft missing

## A2 - Draft Ready

- brief exists or draft exists
- article draft exists
- visual family incomplete

## A3 - Visual Ready

- brief, draft, and cover exist
- optional inline images may still be partial

## A4 - Publish Ready

- brief, draft, and cover exist
- inline images exist if the workflow required them
- naming is consistent across the artifact family
