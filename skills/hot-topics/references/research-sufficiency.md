# Research Sufficiency

Not every trending topic is ready to become a full article lead.

## Good Enough To Escalate

- there is a concrete event, release, or behavior shift
- at least one high-quality source exists
- the topic can support a specific angle instead of vague hype

## Too Thin

- only reposted excitement, no real source
- only headline-level knowledge, no usable detail
- too generic to differentiate from yesterday's topic list

If the topic is too thin, label it as exploratory rather than article-ready.
