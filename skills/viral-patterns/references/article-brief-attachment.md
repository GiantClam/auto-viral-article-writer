# Article Brief Attachment

Pattern retrieval becomes more useful when the output can be attached directly to an article brief.

## Best Attachment Fields

- `title_patterns`
- `emotional_triggers`
- `pattern_summary`
- `structure_suggestions`
- `copy_risk_notes`

## Practical Rule

Do not return pattern findings as a loose dump when the next step is article drafting. Shape them so `write-article` can consume them.
