# Article Archetypes

Choose the article type before writing. The archetype affects the hook, evidence shape, and pacing.

## 1. Investigation or Experiment

Best when the article is built around trying something, testing something, or exposing how a workflow behaves in practice.

Use when:

- there is a real experiment, test, or comparison
- the article benefits from step-by-step discovery

## 2. Product Experience

Best when the article is anchored in direct product use and opinion.

Use when:

- the value comes from real usage impressions
- the article needs scenario-based demonstration

## 3. Phenomenon Analysis

Best when the piece starts from a visible trend, meme, release wave, or behavior shift.

Use when:

- the topic is larger than one tool
- the article needs observation -> explanation -> judgment

## 4. Tool Sharing

Best when the article exists to transfer a useful workflow or capability.

Use when:

- readers need a concrete workflow they can reuse
- the article can be organized around value and use cases

## 5. Methodology or Lessons Learned

Best when the point is to teach a repeatable way of working.

Use when:

- the article is about process, pattern, or accumulated lessons
- each section should end in something actionable
