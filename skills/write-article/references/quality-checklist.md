# Quality Checklist

Before calling the article complete, confirm:

- the topic is clear
- the angle is specific enough to write
- the HKR topic gate passed or the downgrade decision was explicit
- the article archetype was chosen intentionally
- the title candidates are distinct
- section headings are usable for downstream illustration insertion
- claims from external sources are grounded in collected material
- no first-hand details, quotes, or metrics were invented
- the output path is correct

If any image steps fail, keep the draft intact and report that the article is text-complete but image-incomplete.
