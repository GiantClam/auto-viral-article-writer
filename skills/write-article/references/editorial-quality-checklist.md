# Editorial Quality Checklist

Use this after the draft exists but before calling it finished.

## Layer 1 - Hard Checks

- output path is correct
- title candidates are present when required
- section headings are usable for downstream illustration insertion
- obvious unsupported claims are removed or qualified

## Layer 2 - Structure and Platform Fit

- the opening matches the article archetype
- the platform fit is clear
- the pacing is readable for the target platform

## Layer 3 - Content Quality

- the angle is specific, not generic
- claims are grounded in real material
- pattern reuse is adapted rather than copied mechanically

## Layer 4 - Human Readability

- the draft does not feel empty or template-driven
- sections progress with intent
- the article sounds like a person with a real point, not a stitched summary
