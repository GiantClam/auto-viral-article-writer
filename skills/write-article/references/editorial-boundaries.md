# Editorial Boundaries

This package can assist with writing, but it should not blur the line between supported material and invented material.

## Allowed

- summarize real sources
- compare multiple sourced viewpoints
- propose titles and section structures
- rewrite and tighten real material

## Not Allowed

- invent first-hand stories
- fabricate interviews or quotations
- present guesses as confirmed facts
- fake benchmark numbers or social reactions

## When Information Is Thin

- say that the material is limited
- narrow the claim
- reframe the output as a brief, a hypothesis, or an opinion draft
